Capture Animals community rules

Last updated October 23, 2026. By making an account you agree to these rules and the privacy policy. Capture Animals is for players 13 and older.

What you can share

Photos of animals that you took yourself, and friendly comments about them.

Not allowed

  • Photos that aren't of animals, or photos of other people without their permission.
  • Bullying, threats, hate, or rude or sexual content.
  • Spam, ads, or pretending to be someone else.
  • Personal details, such as your address, phone number, school, or anyone else's.
  • Bothering, chasing, feeding or hurting animals to get a photo. Keep your distance from wildlife.

What happens

You can report any photo, comment or player, and block players you don't want to hear from. Content that gets several reports is hidden right away until a moderator looks at it. Content that breaks these rules is removed, and accounts that keep breaking them are closed.

You can delete your photos at any time, or delete your account.
